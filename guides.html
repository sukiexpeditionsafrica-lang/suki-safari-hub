Plan My Safari

Guides are added one at a time as their checks are completed. No guide is listed yet. This page explains the system so travellers know what the badge will mean when guides are added, with approval by Suki Expeditions Africa, one at a time, and the guide's consent.

Verification

Badge levels

Self declared

The guide says they are licensed. No check has been done. No badge.

Peer reviewed

Another guide or operator has vouched for them, or a review exists. Still no licence check. No badge.

Registry verified Verified

The country check below has been done and recorded, and nothing in it has expired. This is the only level that shows the verified badge.

If a document expires, or a suspension is found, the badge comes off until a fresh check is recorded.

By Country

How we check, by country

Kenya

Kenya

Tourism Regulatory Authority, TRA, tour guide licence. Checked on the public portal verify.tra.go.ke, by ID number for a guide, or file number for a company. KPSGA level is a separate professional label, not the licence itself.

Uganda

Uganda

No public individual guide portal was found. We check the Uganda Tourism Board, UTB, operator licence first, with its number, expiry and suspension position. USAGA membership is a separate label, not the licence.

Tanzania

Tanzania, mainland

Ministry of Natural Resources and Tourism, MNRT, Certificate of Registration plus the current year identity card. The certificate renews every third November, the identity card expires every 31 December. Only Tanzanian citizens can be licensed as guides in Tanzania. Zanzibar is not covered by this mainland check yet.

Rwanda

Rwanda

Rwanda Development Board, RDB, tourism operating licence, plus association letter, police clearance within one year and medical certificate within three months. The licence itself has no expiry date under current law.

The Standard

What never counts as verified

Association membership alone, park registration alone, training alone, or a certificate without its companion document in date. National ID, licence and certificate numbers are check inputs, never public display fields. The app shows the badge, the country, the registry name and the check date only.

Joining

How a guide gets added

  1. Suki Expeditions Africa approves the guide, one at a time.
  2. The guide gives consent to be checked and listed.
  3. The country check is done and recorded.
  4. Only if the check passes and is in date, the guide is listed as registry verified.
