Full catalogue on this site

43 famous national parks, zoos and tourist attractions across Uganda, Kenya, Tanzania and Rwanda. See the full list on the Places page.

Uganda (15)

  • Bwindi Impenetrable National Park (National park) : Mountain gorilla trekking. Gorilla permit $800 per person for foreign non-residents, $700 for foreign residents, $600 low season April, May and November, checked 30 Oct 2026 against UWA tariff summaries, recheck UWA before quoting
  • Kibale National Park (National park) : Chimpanzee tracking. Fee varies by activity, check UWA
  • Queen Elizabeth National Park (National park) : Tree climbing lions, Kazinga Channel, Ishasha. Park fee varies, check UWA
  • Murchison Falls National Park (National park) : Nile Falls, big game, boat safaris. Park fee varies, check UWA
  • Kidepo Valley National Park (National park) : Remote savanna, ostriches, Karamoja culture. Park fee varies, check UWA
  • Mgahinga Gorilla National Park (National park) : Gorillas and golden monkeys, Virunga volcanoes. Gorilla permit $800 per person for foreign non-residents, $700 for foreign residents, $600 low season April, May and November, checked 30 Oct 2026 against UWA tariff summaries, recheck UWA before quoting
  • Lake Mburo National Park (National park) : Zebras, walking safaris, close to Kampala. Park fee varies, check UWA
  • Rwenzori Mountains National Park (National park) : Mountains of the Moon hiking. Park fee varies, check UWA
  • Semuliki National Park (National park) : Hot springs, lowland forest birds. Park fee varies, check UWA
  • Mount Elgon National Park (National park) : Waterfalls, caves, hiking. Park fee varies, check UWA
  • Lake Bunyonyi (Tourist attraction) : Island lake, canoeing, relaxation after trekking. No national park fee, local activity prices vary
  • Jinja and the Source of the Nile (Tourist attraction) : White water rafting, Nile boat trips. Activity prices vary
  • Sipi Falls (Tourist attraction) : Waterfalls and coffee tours near Mount Elgon. Local guide fees vary
  • Uganda Wildlife Conservation Education Centre, Entebbe Zoo (Zoo) : Rescued wildlife, education, Lake Victoria shore. Entry fee varies, check centre
  • Kampala City and Kasubi Tombs (Tourist attraction) : City culture, markets, heritage. Site entry fees vary

Kenya (13)

  • Maasai Mara National Reserve (National park) : Great Migration crossings, big cats. Park fee varies by season, check KWS
  • Amboseli National Park (National park) : Elephants with Kilimanjaro views. Park fee varies, check KWS
  • Tsavo East National Park (National park) : Red elephants, Aruba Dam, Lugard Falls. Park fee varies, check KWS
  • Tsavo West National Park (National park) : Mzima Springs, rhino sanctuary. Park fee varies, check KWS
  • Lake Nakuru National Park (National park) : Flamingos, rhinos, lake views. Park fee varies, check KWS
  • Samburu National Reserve (National park) : Special Five, Ewaso Nyiro river. Park fee varies, check KWS
  • Nairobi National Park (National park) : Wildlife against a city skyline. Park fee varies, check KWS
  • Hell's Gate National Park (National park) : Cycling, gorge walking, geothermal landscape. Park fee varies, check KWS
  • Mount Kenya National Park (National park) : Mountain hiking, alpine lakes. Park fee varies, check KWS
  • Diani Beach (Tourist attraction) : White sand beach, diving, water sports. No park fee, hotel and activity prices vary
  • Lamu Old Town (Tourist attraction) : Swahili heritage, dhow trips. Site and activity prices vary
  • Nairobi Animal Orphanage and Giraffe Centre (Zoo) : Giraffe feeding, rescued animals, education. Entry fee varies, check centre
  • David Sheldrick Wildlife Trust, Nairobi (Zoo) : Orphaned elephant care and adoption. Entry fee varies, check trust

Tanzania (10)

  • Serengeti National Park (National park) : Great Migration, endless plains, big cats. Reported $70 plus VAT to $82.60 all in, sources conflict, check TANAPA
  • Ngorongoro Crater and Conservation Area (National park) : Crater wildlife, Maasai culture. Park and crater fees vary, check NCAA
  • Tarangire National Park (National park) : Baobabs, elephant herds. Park fee varies, check TANAPA
  • Lake Manyara National Park (National park) : Tree climbing lions, lake flamingos. Park fee varies, check TANAPA
  • Kilimanjaro National Park (National park) : Mount Kilimanjaro climbing routes. Climbing and park fees vary, check TANAPA
  • Nyerere National Park, formerly Selous (National park) : Boat safaris, wild dogs, remote wilderness. Park fee varies, check TANAPA
  • Ruaha National Park (National park) : Large elephant herds, baobabs, predators. Park fee varies, check TANAPA
  • Arusha National Park (National park) : Mount Meru, Momella Lakes, giraffes. Park fee varies, check TANAPA
  • Zanzibar Beaches (Tourist attraction) : Nungwi and Paje beaches, spice tours. No park fee, plus Zanzibar insurance requirements, check official guidance
  • Stone Town, Zanzibar (Tourist attraction) : Swahili heritage, markets, historic houses. Site entry fees vary

Rwanda (5)

  • Volcanoes National Park (National park) : Mountain gorilla trekking. Gorilla permit $1,500 per person, October 2026 research figure, recheck RDB
  • Akagera National Park (National park) : Big Five, Lake Ihema boat trips. Park fee varies, check RDB
  • Nyungwe Forest National Park (National park) : Chimpanzees, canopy walk, colobus monkeys. Park and activity fees vary, check RDB
  • Lake Kivu (Tourist attraction) : Lake beaches, boat trips, Gisenyi and Kibuye. Activity prices vary
  • Kigali Genocide Memorial (Tourist attraction) : Memorial, history and education. Entry guidance varies, check memorial
