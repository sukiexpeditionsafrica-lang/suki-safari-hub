What this site does

  • Calculator: build a cost estimate for a trip, with permits, park fees, stays, guide and vehicle, transport and a guide tips allowance.
  • Places: all 43 famous parks, zoos and attractions in our catalogue, by country, with what each place is known for and its fee note.
  • Guides: how we check guides, what the verified badge means, and how guides are added one at a time as their checks are completed.

Estimates are estimates, not quotes. Fees change, so recheck park, visa and permit fees on official portals before booking: KWS, TANAPA, NCAA, UWA, RDB and immigration.
