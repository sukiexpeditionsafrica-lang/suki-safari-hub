Why the change

Companies first. Guides second.

Travellers do not buy a safari from a guide. They buy it from an operator, the company that holds the licence, the vehicle, the lodge relationships and the permit access. So that is who the Hub now lists first.

Easier to hold accountable

A company has a registered address, a licence that can be suspended, insurance and a bank account. An individual can change a phone number and disappear.

How safaris are actually bought

The operator plans the trip, secures the gorilla and chimp permits, provides the vehicle and assigns the guide. Listing the operator matches the real booking.

Operators are the supply

An app that looks like it cuts operators out will be fought or ignored. An app that brings them verified demand, trust and safer payment gets adopted.

The badge rule

Only one level earns the badge.

Association membership alone never earns it. A licence for a different country never earns it for this country. An expired or suspended licence loses it.

No badge

Self declared

The operator says it is licensed. No check has been done yet, so it is listed without a badge, or not shown as verified in any way.

Still no badge

Peer reviewed

Another operator or a traveller review has vouched for it. Helpful context, but still no licence check, so still no badge.

Verified

Registry verified

The country licence check below has been done, recorded and is still in date. This is the only level that shows the verified badge.

If a document expires, or a suspension is found, the badge comes off until a fresh check is recorded.

Checked country by country

How we check, by country

An operator working in two countries needs two checks. Tap a country to see exactly what is checked there.

Uganda Tourism Board · UTB

Uganda

Licence checked: UTB Tourism Operating Licence, currently valid, 1 year.

Also recorded: URSB Certificate of Incorporation, current year URA e-Acknowledgement Receipt, current year Annual Return with all shareholders named.

Recheck: at licence expiry each year, plus suspension position at listing time.

Does not count alone: AUTO or USAGA membership, company registration without the UTB licence.

Tourism Regulatory Authority · TRA

Kenya

Licence checked: TRA licence for the company, checked on the public portal at verify.tra.go.ke by company file number.

Also recorded: portal result, licence currency and check date.

Recheck: when the licence is due to expire, and before re-listing after a long gap.

Does not count alone: KATO membership. KPSGA levels belong to individual guides, not the company.

TALA · Mainland Tanzania

Tanzania

Licence checked: TALA licence, number and year. It expires every 31 December.

Also recorded: checked against the TATO online member directory. A TATO listing alone is not a licence check.

Recheck: every year, before 31 December. Zanzibar is not covered by this mainland check.

Does not count alone: TATO membership, or a missing TATO listing treated as a verdict.

Rwanda Development Board · RDB

Rwanda

Licence checked: RDB Tourism Operating Licence (TOL) for the company, certificate sighted, as there is no public portal.

Also recorded: TOL certificate, check date, and suspension or revocation position.

Recheck: suspension or revocation position at listing time.

Does not count alone: RTTA membership, park registration, or a licence from another country.

For operators

How an operator gets listed.

One at a time, by approval. There is no self-service verified badge.

  1. Sula approves the operator, one at a time.
  2. The operator agrees to be checked and listed, and supplies its documents.
  3. The licence check for its country is done and every field is recorded.
  4. Only a passing, in-date check sets the status to registry verified.
  5. The operator is added, showing badge, country, registry and check date.
  6. The operator names its guides; each gets the Tier 2 check.
  7. Checks repeat at expiry. A suspension removes the badge until a fresh check passes.
  8. No money held yet. No payments, escrow or onboarding is live yet. No money is held by Suki.

Questions

Verified operators, explained.

What does verified operator mean on Suki Safari Hub?

It means the operator's tourism operating licence for that country has been checked against the official registry or documents, recorded with a date, and is still in date. In Uganda that is the UTB licence, in Kenya the TRA licence, in mainland Tanzania the TALA licence, and in Rwanda the RDB licence.

Does association membership make an operator verified?

No. Membership of KATO, AUTO, TATO, RTTA or USAGA is shown as a separate label only. Membership alone never earns the verified badge in any country.

Can I still see who my guide will be?

Yes. Each verified operator names the guides it assigns. Those guides are checked under Tier 2 and shown under the operator, which is responsible for them during your safari.

Why is Suki Expeditions Africa not verified on its own Hub?

Because the same rule applies to Suki as to everyone else. Suki's UTB licence and supporting documents must be checked and recorded first. Until then its listing honestly shows as verification pending, with no badge.

What happens if an operator's licence expires or is suspended?

The badge comes off, the status returns to self declared, and its named guides are hidden until a fresh check is recorded. The operator record is kept, with the reason and date noted, it is not deleted.
